## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: No demos scheduled
**Mitigation**: 
- Increase outbound volume (20→40 calls/day)
- Offer free compliance audit
- Partner with consultants for referrals

### Risk 2: Trials don't convert
**Mitigation**:
- More hands-on onboarding
- Success criteria realignment
- Extend trial to 45 days
- Offer implementation support

### Risk 3: Pricing too high/low
**Mitigation**:
- A/B test pricing ($8K vs $12K vs custom)
- Offer quarterly payment option
- Create "Starter Enterprise" tier at $5K

### Risk 4: Missing key features (SSO)
**Mitigation**:
- Roadmap commitment (SSO in Q2)
- Pilot pricing discount
- Workaround documentation

## Templates & Scripts

### Email: Cold Outreach (IT/Compliance Officer)

```
Subject: Survey compliance question for [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

Quick question: When your team runs employee/customer surveys, 
how do you track who accessed the data for GDPR/compliance purposes?

Most survey tools I've seen don't have audit trails, which causes 
headaches during compliance reviews.

We just built a solution specifically for [Industry] with:
- Complete audit logs (who, what, when, IP address)
- Role-based access control with three levels: admin, editor, viewer
- Fine-grained permissions using action codes (e.g., `questionnaires:delete`, `workspace:billing`)
- Data retention controls
- Workspace isolation for multi-tenant security

Would you be open to a 15-minute demo to see if this solves 
your compliance challenges?

[Your name]
```

### Demo Script: Audit Logs (10 minutes)

```
1. Setup (2 min)
   "Let me show you the biggest compliance challenge we solve..."
   *Open sample questionnaire with sensitive data*

2. The Problem (2 min)
   "Imagine your compliance officer asks: 
    'Who viewed the employee salary survey last month?'"
   "With most tools, you can't answer that. Let me show you how we can."

3. The Solution (4 min)
   *Navigate to /dashboard/workspace/settings/audit*
   "Here's every action in your workspace:"
   - Point out user IDs
   - Highlight timestamps
   - Show IP addresses
   - Demonstrate filtering by action type
   
   "You can export this for compliance audits."

4. ROI (2 min)
   "What's the cost of a GDPR violation? Up to 4% of revenue.
    Our platform gives you the audit trail to prove compliance."
    
   "Questions?"
```
