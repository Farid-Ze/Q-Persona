**90-Day Enterprise Launch Plan**

Comprehensive execution plan to transform Q-Persona from technical platform to revenue-generating enterprise business.

**Goal**: Launch Enterprise tier and close first 3 enterprise deals within 90 days

**Key Phases:**
1. **Phase 1: Foundation** (Days 1-30)
   - Product packaging and positioning
   - Marketing materials creation
   - Sales enablement

2. **Phase 2: First Deals** (Days 31-60)
   - Outbound campaigns
   - Demo scheduling
   - Pilot programs

3. **Phase 3: Scale** (Days 61-90)
   - Sales playbook documentation
   - Team expansion
   - Referral program launch

- **30-Day**: $0 → Enterprise page live, 10 demos scheduled

## 💼 Resource Requirements

### Team
- **Sales**: 1 Enterprise AE (hire Month 2)
- **Marketing**: 1 Content/Demand Gen
- **Product**: 1 PM for enterprise features
- **Engineering**: 1-2 developers for support
- **Customer Success**: 1 CSM (hire Month 3)

### Budget Allocation
- **Marketing**: $10K/month (content, ads, events)
- **Sales Tools**: $2K/month (CRM, outreach tools)
- **Product**: $5K/month (infrastructure, tools)
- **Total**: $17K/month for 90 days = $51K

### ROI Projection
- **Investment**: $51K
- **Revenue (90 days)**: $60K ARR
- **Payback Period**: ~10 months
- **Year 1 ROI**: 9x ($460K ARR)
