## Contact Research Template

For each target account, research and document:

**Company Intel:**
- Recent news (mergers, audits, incidents)
- Tech stack (LinkedIn engineering posts)
- Compliance requirements (industry)
- Survey usage indicators (job postings, vendor lists)

**Decision Maker:**
- Name, title, LinkedIn profile
- Background, tenure
- Pain points (from posts, articles)
- Warm intro path (mutual connections)

**Timing:**
- Budget cycle (typically Q4 for next year)
- Audit schedule (quarterly, annual)
- Current contract expiry (if known)
- Trigger events (leadership change, audit failure)

## Account Scoring Model

Assign score 0-10 based on:
- **Fit:** Industry match, compliance needs (0-3)
- **Intent:** Active search signals (0-3)
- **Reach:** Warm intro available (0-2)
- **Timing:** Budget/urgency (0-2)

**Score 8-10:** Immediate outreach (High priority)  
**Score 5-7:** Schedule outreach (Medium priority)  
**Score 0-4:** Nurture (Low priority)
