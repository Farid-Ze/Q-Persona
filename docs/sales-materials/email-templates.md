## Email Metrics & Best Practices

### Open Rate Benchmarks
- **Cold outreach:** 20-30% (target: >25%)
- **Demo follow-up:** 40-60% (target: >50%)
- **Trial emails:** 60-80% (target: >70%)

### Response Rate Benchmarks
- **Cold outreach:** 5-10% (target: >8%)
- **Demo follow-up:** 20-40% (target: >30%)
- **Trial check-ins:** 30-50% (target: >40%)

### Conversion Benchmarks
- **Demo → Trial:** 50% (target: >60%)
- **Trial → Paid:** 30% (target: >40%)
- **Business → Enterprise:** 20% (target: >25%)

### Best Practices

**Subject Lines:**
- Keep under 50 characters
- Ask questions (higher open rates)
- Mention their company name
- Avoid "sales-y" words (free, urgent, limited time)

**Body:**
- Get to the point in first 2 lines
- One ask per email
- Use white space (short paragraphs)
- Always include a clear CTA

**Timing:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am local time
- Follow up 3 business days after no response
- Max 3 follow-ups before pausing

**Personalization:**
- Use their name (First Name only)
- Reference their industry/company
- Mention specific pain points from discovery
- Show you've done research
