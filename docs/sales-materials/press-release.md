## Q-Persona Launches Enterprise Tier for Regulated Industries  
### New Platform Features Complete Audit Trail, SSO, and MFA to Help Banks, Healthcare, and Government Pass Compliance Audits

**Jakarta, Indonesia – [Date]** – Q-Persona, Indonesia's leading survey platform, today announced the launch of its Enterprise tier, purpose-built for regulated industries requiring complete compliance and security controls.

The new Enterprise offering includes features previously unavailable in the Indonesian survey market: complete audit trails with unlimited retention, Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and a 99.5% uptime SLA—all critical requirements for banks, healthcare organizations, and government agencies.

### The Compliance Gap in Survey Tools

"We discovered a massive gap in the market," said [Founder Name], CEO of Q-Persona. "Banks and hospitals were trying to use consumer survey tools for compliance-critical work. When auditors asked, 'Can you show us who accessed this patient survey data?' they had no answer. That's a Rp 5 billion regulatory violation waiting to happen."

Traditional survey platforms like SurveyMonkey and Google Forms were designed for marketing teams, not regulated industries. They lack enterprise-grade security controls, making them unsuitable for collecting sensitive customer, patient, or employee data.

### Enterprise Features That Pass IT Security Review

Q-Persona's Enterprise tier addresses this gap with four critical capabilities:

**1. Complete Audit Trail (The Golden Key)**  
Every action is logged with timestamp, user ID, IP address, and user-agent—exportable for compliance auditors. This alone enables banks to pass OJK and BI audits, hospitals to demonstrate HIPAA compliance, and government agencies to meet data governance requirements.

**2. Single Sign-On (SSO) Integration**  
SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0 support for Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and custom identity providers. IT teams can now centralize authentication and instantly deprovision access when employees leave—a critical security control.

**3. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**  
TOTP authenticator apps and SMS verification prevent 99.9% of account takeovers. MFA can be enforced for all workspace members, meeting the security requirements of SOC 2 and ISO 27001.

**4. Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure**  
99.5% uptime SLA, dedicated success manager, priority support, and custom contract terms. Built on Supabase (SOC 2 Type II certified) with data residency options in Singapore, EU, and US regions.
