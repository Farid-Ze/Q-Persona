## Executive Summary

This playbook documents the proven enterprise sales process for Q-Persona, optimized for selling to regulated industries (banking, healthcare, government). Use this as the definitive guide for closing enterprise deals consistently.

**Target Close Rate:** 25% (1 in 4 qualified demos converts)  
**Average Sales Cycle:** 45-60 days

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary Targets (80% of effort)

**Banking & Financial Services (40% of pipeline)**
- **Company Size:** 200+ employees
- **Decision Makers:** IT Director, Compliance Officer, CISO
- **Pain Points:** 
  - Failed compliance audits due to lack of audit trails
  - Bank Indonesia/OJK regulatory requirements
  - GDPR compliance for international operations
- **Budget Authority:** $10K-50K/year for compliance tools
- **Buying Signals:** Recent audit findings, new compliance officer, regulatory changes

**Healthcare Providers (30% of pipeline)**
- **Company Size:** 100+ employees or 50+ beds
- **Decision Makers:** IT Manager, Privacy Officer, Medical Director
- **Pain Points:**
  - HIPAA compliance for patient surveys
  - Need for Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
  - PHI protection requirements
- **Budget Authority:** $8K-30K/year for healthcare IT
- **Buying Signals:** HIPAA audit scheduled, new EHR implementation, patient survey needs

**Large Enterprises (20% of pipeline)**
- **Company Size:** 500+ employees
- **Decision Makers:** HR Director, IT Security, Procurement
- **Pain Points:**
  - Employee survey tools don't pass IT security reviews
  - Need SSO integration
  - Multi-department workspace isolation
- **Budget Authority:** $15K-50K/year for SaaS tools
- **Buying Signals:** Annual engagement survey season, IT tool consolidation

**Research Institutions (10% of pipeline)**
- **Company Size:** Universities, think tanks, research firms
- **Decision Makers:** Research Director, IT, Compliance
- **Pain Points:**
  - Multi-country compliance (GDPR + local)
  - Data sovereignty requirements
  - IRB approval needs
- **Budget Authority:** $5K-20K/year for research tools
- **Buying Signals:** Grant funding received, new research project, publication requirements

### Disqualifiers (Don't Pursue)
- ❌ Companies <50 employees (not enterprise ready)
- ❌ No compliance/security requirements (use SMB tier)
- ❌ Budget <$5K/year (not enterprise fit)
- ❌ No clear decision maker identified
- ❌ Using free tools with no budget allocated

## 2. Lead Qualification Framework (BANT)

**Use this before scheduling demos to qualify leads:**

### Budget
- ✅ **Qualified:** Budget allocated ($10K+/year for tools)
- 🟡 **Maybe:** Budget discussion needed (can reallocate from existing tool)
- ❌ **Disqualified:** No budget, <$5K total

### Authority
- ✅ **Qualified:** Speaking with decision maker (Director+) or champion with budget influence
- 🟡 **Maybe:** Mid-level manager who can influence decision
- ❌ **Disqualified:** Junior employee with no influence

### Need
- ✅ **Qualified:** Active compliance pain (failed audit, upcoming review, regulatory requirement)
- 🟡 **Maybe:** General interest in better tools
- ❌ **Disqualified:** Just browsing, no specific need

### Timeline
- ✅ **Qualified:** Buying within 60 days (audit scheduled, fiscal year budget)
- 🟡 **Maybe:** 90-120 day timeline
- ❌ **Disqualified:** "Just researching" with no timeline

**Qualification Script:**
```
"Thanks for your interest! To make sure I can best help you, I have a few quick questions:

1. What's driving your need for a new survey platform right now? (Need)
2. Who else would be involved in evaluating and approving a tool like this? (Authority)
3. When are you looking to have a solution in place? (Timeline)
4. What budget range have you allocated for this? (Budget)

[Based on answers, either schedule demo or provide resources]"
```

## 3. Outreach Strategies That Work

### What Works (Do More of This)

**LinkedIn Outreach (Best ROI)**
- ✅ Connect with personalized note mentioning mutual connection or specific pain point
- ✅ Follow up 3-5 days later with value (article, whitepaper)
- ✅ Third touch: Soft demo ask ("Would a 15-min overview be helpful?")
- **Success Rate:** 15% connection → 5% demo booking
- **Best Days:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am local time

**Email Sequences (Steady Pipeline)**
- ✅ Email 1: Problem statement (compliance challenge)
- ✅ Email 2: Social proof (case study or testimonial)
- ✅ Email 3: Specific value prop (audit logs walkthrough)
- ✅ Email 4: Time-limited offer (Q4 discount, early access)
- **Success Rate:** 2-3% demo booking from cold list
- **Best Times:** Tuesday/Wednesday 8-10am

**Warm Introductions (Highest Close Rate)**
- ✅ Ask existing customers for referrals
- ✅ Partner with compliance consultants
- ✅ Network at industry events (banking, healthcare conferences)
- **Success Rate:** 40% demo → 25% close
- **Best Source:** Customer referrals

### What Doesn't Work (Stop Doing This)
- ❌ Generic mass emails ("Dear Sir/Madam")
- ❌ Cold calling without research
- ❌ Selling features instead of solving problems
- ❌ Asking for 60-minute demos (too long for first call)
- ❌ Targeting consumer/SMB companies with enterprise messaging

## 4. Demo Excellence Framework

### The Perfect 30-Minute Demo Structure

**Minutes 0-5: Discovery & Rapport**
- "Tell me about your current survey process"
- "What triggered your search for a new solution?"
- "What would success look like for you?"
- **Goal:** Understand their specific pain, build trust

**Minutes 5-10: Problem Amplification**
- Restate their pain point back to them
- "So if I heard you correctly, your biggest challenge is [X]"
- Share similar customer story (without naming names yet)
- **Goal:** Make them feel understood, establish credibility

**Minutes 10-20: The Golden Key Demo (Audit Logs)**
- Navigate to live audit log interface
- Show filtered view: "Here's who viewed your salary survey last month"
- Demonstrate export: "This PDF goes straight to your auditor"
- Filter by action type, date range, user
- **Goal:** Prove we solve their #1 pain point

**Minutes 20-25: Supporting Features**
- Quick tour of SSO configuration (if applicable)
- Role-based access control demonstration
- MFA enforcement settings
- **Goal:** Address IT security checklist items

**Minutes 25-30: Next Steps & Close**
- "Based on what you've seen, does this solve [pain point]?"
- "What concerns do you still have?"
- "Would a 30-day trial make sense for your team?"
- Schedule follow-up or trial setup call
- **Goal:** Get commitment to next step

### Demo Do's and Don'ts

**✅ Do:**
- Start with their specific use case
- Use their company name in examples when possible
- Pause for questions frequently
- Screen share with clean, professional setup
- Have customer references ready

**❌ Don't:**
- Show every feature (focus on pain points)
- Rush through audit logs (it's the killer feature)
- Ignore their questions to finish script
- Demo on a messy/test account
- Promise features not yet built

## 5. Objection Handling Scripts

### "Too Expensive" ($10K/year is a lot)

**Response:**
```
"I understand budget is always a consideration. Let me share some context:

The average GDPR fine is €25 million. Even a small compliance violation 
can cost Rp 200 million in remediation.

Our ROI calculator shows that for your size organization, Q-Persona 
saves approximately Rp 550 million per year in compliance risk alone.

The question isn't whether you can afford Q-Persona—it's whether you 
can afford NOT to have complete audit trails when the next audit comes.

That said, if budget is truly a constraint right now, we could discuss:
1. Starting with a pilot in one department
2. Quarterly payment terms to spread the cost
3. A phased rollout over 6 months

What makes most sense for your situation?"
```

**If they still resist:** Offer Business tier trial ($99/month) to prove value, then upgrade path.

### "We need SSO" (already have SSO)

**Response:**
```
"Great news—SSO is included in our Enterprise tier. We support:
- SAML 2.0 (Okta, Azure AD, OneLogin)
- OAuth 2.0 (Google Workspace)
- Automatic user provisioning

Most implementations take less than 30 minutes. I can have our 
solutions engineer join our next call to walk through the 
configuration if you'd like."
```

**Action:** Schedule technical deep-dive call with customer's IT team.

### "Need to think about it" (buying delay)

**Response:**
```
"Absolutely, this is an important decision. To help with your evaluation, 
can I ask what specific concerns you need to think through?

[Listen to their answer]

Based on that, let me suggest: Why don't we set up a 30-day trial 
for your team? That way you can:
1. Test it with real surveys
2. Get your IT team's security review
3. See the audit logs in action with your data

We can schedule a check-in call in 2 weeks to address any questions 
that come up. Does that sound helpful?"
```

**Goal:** Convert thinking into trial action.

### "Can't get budget approved" (no budget authority)

**Response:**
```
"I understand budget approval can be complex. Let me help make the 
case to your leadership:

I can provide you with:
1. ROI calculator showing $550K/year savings
2. Executive one-pager (1-page business case)
3. Security whitepaper for IT review
4. Compliance datasheet for legal/compliance team

Additionally, many of our customers start with a pilot:
- One department (e.g., HR for employee surveys)
- $10K investment
- 90-day proof of concept
- Then expand based on success

Would it help if I joined your budget discussion call to answer 
technical questions?"
```

**Action:** Offer to support their internal selling process.

### "Missing key features" (feature gap)

**Response:**
```
"Thank you for bringing that up. Can you tell me more about 
[specific feature] and how you'd use it?

[Listen to use case]

Here's our situation:
1. [Feature] is on our Q2 2024 roadmap
2. We're prioritizing based on customer input
3. Early adopters get feature input and early access

In the meantime, here are some options:
- API/webhook integration as a workaround
- Manual process for now, automated later
- Prioritize your feature in our roadmap

Would you be open to being a design partner for [feature]? 
You'd get early access and direct input on the implementation."
```

**Action:** Document feature request, involve product team if critical.

## 6. Trial Success Framework

### 30-Day Trial Milestones

**Day 1: Onboarding Call (30 minutes)**
- ✅ Setup workspace and branding
- ✅ Configure SSO (if applicable)
- ✅ Import team members
- ✅ Create first questionnaire together
- ✅ Schedule Day 7 check-in

**Day 7: Progress Check-In (20 minutes)**
- ✅ Review usage (# of questionnaires, responses)
- ✅ Identify blockers or questions
- ✅ Demo advanced features they haven't tried
- ✅ Show audit logs with their actual data
- ✅ Schedule Day 14 review

**Day 14: Success Review (30 minutes)**
- ✅ Present usage metrics and value delivered
- ✅ Introduce IT/Legal for security review (if needed)
- ✅ Discuss compliance benefits achieved
- ✅ Share customer success story similar to their use case
- ✅ Schedule Day 21 decision call

**Day 21: Closing Call (45 minutes)**
- ✅ Present ROI analysis with their actual usage
- ✅ Address remaining concerns
- ✅ Negotiate contract terms if needed
- ✅ Get verbal commitment
- ✅ Send contract and schedule signature call

**Day 30: Convert or Extend**
- Convert to paid subscription, OR
- Extend trial by 15 days (if close to decision), OR
- Archive as "not now" with 6-month follow-up

### Trial Success Metrics

**Green (Likely to Convert):**
- 3+ active users
- 5+ questionnaires created
- 50+ responses collected
- IT security review passed
- Regular platform login activity

**Yellow (Needs Attention):**
- 1-2 users active
- 1-3 questionnaires
- Irregular login activity
- Security review pending

**Red (At Risk):**
- Only 1 user (trial sponsor)
- 0-1 questionnaires
- No responses collected
- No login activity in 7+ days
- **Action:** Urgent call to re-engage

## 7. Closing Techniques

### The Assumptive Close
```
"Based on our conversations and your trial results, I'll go ahead and 
prepare the contract for [X users] at our Enterprise tier. 

When would you like to officially kick off—beginning of next month 
or right away?"
```
**When to use:** Strong trial success, clear buying signals

### The Alternative Close
```
"I'm seeing two options that make sense for you:

Option A: Enterprise tier with unlimited audit logs ($10K/year)
Option B: Business tier with 30-day logs, upgrade later ($1,188/year)

Which aligns better with your compliance requirements?"
```
**When to use:** Budget concerns but clear need

### The Urgency Close
```
"I want to be transparent with you: We're running a Q4 promotion 
through November 30th—20% off first year for new enterprise customers.

That brings your investment down from $10K to $8K for the first year, 
saving you $2,000.

To lock in this pricing, we'd need to have the contract signed by 
the 30th. Can we target that timeline?"
```
**When to use:** Decision paralysis, need gentle push

### The Referral Close
```
"[Name], I'm confident Q-Persona will solve your compliance challenges 
based on what I've seen in your trial.

Here's what I propose: Let's move forward with the contract, and if 
you're happy after 90 days, I'd love an introduction to [other department 
or similar company].

Does that sound fair?"
```
**When to use:** Strong relationship, expansion opportunity

### Health Score Metrics

- Questionnaires deployed monthly (20 pts)
- Responses being collected (20 pts)

**Red (Churn Risk - <50 points):**

**User Growth:**
- Monitor: Number of active users approaching tier limit
- Trigger: 80% of user limit reached
- Action: "I noticed you're at 40 of 50 users. Let's discuss upgrading to accommodate your growth."

**Response Volume:**
- Monitor: Monthly response count trending up
- Trigger: 80% of monthly limit used
- Action: "Your survey participation is great! Let's talk about a higher tier to avoid hitting limits."

**Department Expansion:**
- Monitor: Multiple departments requesting access
- Trigger: 3+ departments using platform
- Action: "I see HR, Marketing, and Operations all using Q-Persona. Enterprise tier would give you unlimited workspaces for each department."

**Feature Needs:**
- Monitor: Support tickets requesting enterprise features
- Trigger: Request for unlimited audit logs, custom SLA, etc.

## 13. Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake #1: Selling to Unqualified Leads
**Problem:** Wasting time on companies with no budget/authority/need  
**Solution:** Use BANT qualification before scheduling demos

### ❌ Mistake #2: Feature Dumping in Demos
**Problem:** Showing every feature instead of solving specific pain  
**Solution:** Focus 70% of demo time on audit logs (the golden key)

### ❌ Mistake #3: No Trial Follow-Up Plan
**Problem:** Start trial but don't check in, customer goes dark  
**Solution:** Scheduled check-ins on Days 7, 14, 21, 30

### ❌ Mistake #4: Giving Up Too Early
**Problem:** One "no" and move on  
**Solution:** Average enterprise deal needs 5-7 touchpoints

### ❌ Mistake #5: Not Asking for Referrals
**Problem:** Closed customer but didn't ask for introductions  
**Solution:** Ask during QBR or after success milestone

### ❌ Mistake #6: Weak Objection Handling
**Problem:** Accept price objection without showing ROI  
**Solution:** Use ROI calculator, show compliance risk cost

### ❌ Mistake #7: No Multi-Threading
**Problem:** Only talking to one person (single point of failure)  
**Solution:** Engage IT, Compliance, and Business stakeholders

### ❌ Mistake #8: Ignoring Customer Success
**Problem:** Close deal, hand off to CS, customer churns  
**Solution:** Stay involved through first QBR, smooth handoff
