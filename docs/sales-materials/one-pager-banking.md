## The Challenge

Financial institutions need to collect customer and employee feedback while maintaining strict compliance with:
- **Bank Indonesia regulations** - Complete audit trails for customer data
- **OJK (Financial Services Authority)** - Data access logging and governance
- **GDPR** - For international operations and EU customers
- **Internal Audit Requirements** - Demonstrable data protection

**The Problem:** Traditional survey tools like Google Forms, SurveyMonkey, and Typeform don't provide the audit trails required to pass compliance reviews.

**The Impact:**
- ❌ Failed compliance audits
- ❌ Regulatory fines (up to Rp 5 billion under PDP Law)
- ❌ Manual log reconstruction (100+ hours per audit)
- ❌ Shadow IT risk (employees using unapproved tools)
