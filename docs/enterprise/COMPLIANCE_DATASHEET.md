## 🌍 GDPR Compliance (General Data Protection Regulation)

**Status**: ✅ Fully Compliant

### Data Subject Rights

| Right | Implementation | How We Support |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Right to Access | ✅ Full Support | Users can export all their personal data via dashboard |
| Right to Erasure | ✅ Full Support | Account deletion with complete data purge within 30 days |
| Right to Rectification | ✅ Full Support | Users can update personal information at any time |
| Right to Portability | ✅ Full Support | Export data in JSON, CSV formats |
| Right to Object | ✅ Full Support | Opt-out mechanisms for data processing |
| Right to Restriction | ✅ Full Support | Ability to limit specific data processing activities |
| Right to be Informed | ✅ Full Support | Clear privacy policy and data usage notifications |

### GDPR Principles

**1. Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency**
- ✅ Clear privacy policy explaining data use
- ✅ Consent mechanisms for data collection
- ✅ Transparent data processing practices

**2. Purpose Limitation**
- ✅ Data used only for stated purposes
- ✅ No secondary use without consent
- ✅ Purpose documented in privacy policy

**3. Data Minimization**
- ✅ Collect only necessary data
- ✅ No excessive data collection
- ✅ Regular data cleanup processes

**4. Accuracy**
- ✅ Users can update their data
- ✅ Data validation on input
- ✅ Regular data quality checks

**5. Storage Limitation**
- ✅ Configurable data retention policies
- ✅ Automatic deletion after retention period
- ✅ Legal hold capabilities (Enterprise)

**6. Integrity and Confidentiality**
- ✅ AES-256 encryption at rest
- ✅ TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
- ✅ Access controls and audit logging

**7. Accountability**
- ✅ Data Protection Officer designated
- ✅ Data Processing Agreement (DPA) available
- ✅ Regular compliance audits
- ✅ Incident response procedures

### GDPR Documentation

**Available Documents**:
- ✅ Privacy Policy
- ✅ Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- ✅ Sub-processor List
- ✅ Cookie Policy
- ✅ Data Breach Notification Procedure
- ✅ Data Subject Request Process

**Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**:

### Cross-Border Data Transfers

### Breach Notification

**Process**:
1. **Detection**: Continuous monitoring for security events
2. **Assessment**: Determine if breach qualifies as reportable (within 24 hours)
3. **Notification to DPA**: Within 72 hours of awareness
4. **Notification to Users**: Without undue delay if high risk
5. **Documentation**: Maintain breach register

**Timeline**:
- Internal team notified: Within 1 hour
- Initial assessment: Within 24 hours
- DPA notification: Within 72 hours
- User notification: Within 72 hours (if required)
